Connecting Rural Roots With Urban Opportunities

Connecting People • Skills • Services • Products • Markets Across Villages & Cities

Grameena Utpanna Kendra is an organisation formed by like-minded people to build strong grassroots institutions and create sustainable livelihood opportunities.

We connect people with skills, enterprise, local products, markets and community support.

WHAT WE DO

Create Livelihoods

Develop practical employment and self-employment opportunities for rural households, women, youth, farmers and local workers.

Promote Local Products

Support the promotion and sale of home-made, agricultural and locally produced products.

Connect to Markets

Create opportunities for local producers and service providers to reach customers, institutions and wider markets.

Empower Women

Support skilled rural women in home-based production, food preparation, catering and other income-generating activities.

Build Local Institutions

Organise people, strengthen skills and encourage collective action for sustainable community development.
